## Ask

1. What should the label say? Default: the channel or brand name, in capitals,
   from `profile/about.md` or else `profile/vocabulary.md`.
2. Which corner? Top-left (default), top-right, bottom-left or bottom-right.
3. Leave it off which parts? The intro and outro (default), or nothing.

## Write

- Corner badge for the whole video: small "{label}" label in the {corner}. White
  bold text on a dark rounded box, readable but subtle. Keep it inside the platform
  safe area ({below the top app bar for a top corner | above the bottom app buttons
  for a bottom corner}) and never overlapping captions, my face, or motion cards.
  [Leave it off the {parts}.]

## Conflicts

- Captions, hook text or a logo placed in the same corner. If the user keeps the
  choice, ask which corner the badge moves to instead.
- Another label or watermark shown for the whole video. If the user keeps the
  choice, remove the other one.
